Our Story

Authentic Italian Excellence

Welcome to L'ARTISTA, Riga's destination for authentic Italian cuisine, warm service and carefully crafted plates.

At L'Artista, every meal is treated like a small work of art. In the historic streets of Riga Old Town, our restaurant brings together Italian flavor, fresh ingredients and the beauty of local hospitality.

Grazie for dining with us. We invite you to relax, indulge and enjoy honest Italian food: handmade pasta, expressive pizza, seafood, seasonal starters and classic desserts.
